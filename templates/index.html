Agricultural Soil Health Standards & Guidelines

Official ICAR, USDA-NRCS & FAO parameter classification thresholds

🌱

Available Nitrogen (N)

< 280 kg/ha: Low / Deficient
280 - 560 kg/ha: Medium / Optimal
> 560 kg/ha: High / Surfeit

Drives vegetative biomass and green leaf chlorophyll synthesis. Nitrogen deficiency causes stunted growth and chlorosis (yellowing) in older leaves.

🌾

Available Phosphorus (P₂O₅)

< 23 kg/ha: Low
23 - 56 kg/ha: Medium / Optimal
> 56 kg/ha: High

Stimulates root elongation, early plant vigor, ATP energy metabolism, and flowering. Fixation is severe below pH 5.5 and above pH 8.0.

🛡️

Available Potassium (K₂O)

< 140 kg/ha: Low
140 - 280 kg/ha: Medium / Optimal
> 280 kg/ha: High

Controls stomatal conductance, water stress regulation, lodging resistance, and disease defense. Critical during grain development.

🧪

Soil pH (Reaction)

< 6.0: Acidic (Lime needed)
6.0 - 7.5: Ideal / Neutral
> 8.0: Alkaline / Sodic

Controls microbial enzyme activity and nutrient bioavailability. Acidic soils induce Al/Mn toxicity, while alkaline soils precipitate Fe and Zn.

🍂

Organic Carbon (OC %)

< 0.50%: Low (Depleted)
0.50 - 0.75%: Medium
> 0.75%: High (Fertile)

The cornerstone of soil physical structure, cation exchange capacity (CEC), moisture holding capacity, and microbiological abundance.

⚡

Electrical Conductivity (EC)

< 1.0 dS/m: Normal / Non-saline
1.0 - 2.0 dS/m: Critical
> 2.0 dS/m: Injurious (Saline)

Measures the concentration of soluble electrolyte salts. High salinity induces osmotic drought stress in crop roots even under moist soil conditions.
